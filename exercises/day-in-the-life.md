# Day in the Life of an Automater

Experience a typical day as an automation engineer using Ansible Automation Platform. This workshop combines all three AAP Roadshow modules — Introspection, Standardization, and Operational Efficiency — into a single comprehensive session. Attendees will work through real-world automation scenarios covering backup, infrastructure awareness, reporting, standardization, governance, and scaling automation.

## Who is this workshop best for?

Systems administrators, DevOps engineers, and IT operations teams who want to understand how Ansible Automation Platform fits into daily automation workflows. This workshop is best suited for attendees who have limited or no exposure to Ansible Automation Platform and want to see real-world scenarios across Linux and Windows infrastructure.

## Target audience

IT operations engineers, systems administrators, and anyone interested in enterprise automation across hybrid infrastructure.

## Attendee Prerequisites

* Basic understanding of Linux systems administration
* Familiarity with YAML syntax
* No prior Ansible experience required
