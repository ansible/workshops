# Ansible Automates Events

Ansible Automates in-person events are full-day, one-track conferences that take the best AnsibleFest content and present it to regional audiences around the world. Ansible experts and industry thought leaders will present on trending topics focused around automation. Crowds of 150–400 people are expected for those interested in learning more about IT automation solutions with Ansible.

Ansible Automates virtual conferences are half-day, online events that present a scaled-down version of the content typically presented at in-person events. Virtual Ansible Automates events typically attract more than 1,000 registrations.

## Target audience

IT decision-makers, DevOps leads & engineers, Developers, Enterprise Architects, Operations staff, Operations IT leaders, Network engineers, Systems engineers, Systems administrators, Security professionals, Release engineers, Business leaders.

## Attendee Prerequisites

None.

## Session content selection

Please refer to the [Content Repository](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1iF0-3kgabDvYZqPPxUfaPLvuD1WgQNWS_Hp9FZOaoU4/edit?usp=sharing) to select sessions for your event. If you do not see a session that you would like to run, work with your RDC program lead and reach out to Elle Lathram with your proposed title and abstract for approval.

## Sample in-person agenda

Agendas can be adjusted to suit your event and time constraints as needed.

**MORNING SESSIONS (8:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.)**

* 8:00 – 9:00 AM | Registration & networking
* 9:00 – 9:15 AM | Welcome
* 9:15 – 10:00 AM | Keynote session (select from Content Repository)
* 10:00 – 10:15 AM | Break
* 10:15 – 11:00 AM | Breakout session 1
* 11:00 – 11:15 AM | Break
* 11:15 – 12:00 PM | Breakout session 2

**AFTERNOON SESSIONS (1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.)**

* 1:00 – 1:45 PM | Breakout session 3
* 1:45 – 2:00 PM | Break
* 2:00 – 2:45 PM | Breakout session 4
* 2:45 – 3:00 PM | Break
* 3:00 – 3:45 PM | Breakout session 5
* 3:45 – 4:00 PM | Break
* 4:00 – 4:45 PM | Closing keynote / panel
* 4:45 – 5:00 PM | Wrap-up
