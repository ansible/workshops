# Automation Connection

This account-specific, 3-hour IT automation event is designed to be used as an account upsell / expansion program. It's challenging to reach all the relevant IT automation users within one account. This event is designed to create a space for interaction and community building within the account, across different customer use cases and personas.

## Target audience

IT infrastructure engineers, DevOps engineers, operations teams, and automation champions within the account.

## Presentation Deck

- [Event kit (Google Slides)](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1OQM1mo7KejZrI8hsEK_9ynWJ_2YHXsOTKVzBBze6ulE/edit?usp=sharing) - For Red Hat employees

## Benefits

* Customer champion becomes Red Hat evangelist within the account
* Involvement of multi-level management
* Develop new and broader contacts across different levels and departments within the account
* Helps easily meet an Ansible Automation Platform sales best practice of selling both top-down and bottom-up
* Opens the door for other opportunities
* Access the new groups in the account (Architects, Networking, Windows, Developers community, CIO-Office)

## Sample agenda

**GENERAL SESSION (9:00 AM – 10:00 AM)**

* Opening Keynote + Strategy — Presented by the customer's sponsor
* Keynote Red Hat Automation — Presented by Red Hat

**BREAK-OUT SESSIONS (10:15 AM – 12:30 PM)** — Section to be customized to account's use case interests

* Hands-on Ansible for Windows — Presented by Red Hat Solution Architect
* Ansible for Networking hands-on Demo — Presented by the Red Hat Senior Specialist Solution Architect
* Transformative automation: Be an automation champion — Presented by the Red Hat Consulting and Training managers
* Advanced Ansible Tips & Tricks — Presented by Red Hat Solution Architect

## When to run Automation Connection

Selection consideration stage — designed to upsell/expand new or existing accounts strategies.
