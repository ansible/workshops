# Mission-Critical Automation Workshop

The Mission-Critical Automation Workshop is a dynamic 3.5-hour hands-on experience designed to introduce participants to Ansible Automation Platform and to immerse participants in the journey of a new automator joining a team, progressing from using automation for information gathering to maturing their automation journey and building responsive automation that prevents and mitigates outages, minimizes downtime, and ensures mission-critical workloads remain operational.

## Who is this workshop best for?

This workshop is tailored for individuals with limited or no experience in writing Ansible Playbooks or using Ansible Automation Platform. It's perfect for IT infrastructure engineers, DevOps engineers, and operations teams eager to experience the automation journey.

## Target audience

IT infrastructure engineers, DevOps engineers, and operations teams eager to experience the automation journey.

## Topics covered

* **Introspection:** Infrastructure Awareness and Reporting
* **Standardization:** Configuration and Application Deployment
* **Operational Efficiency:** Mission Critical Automation with Event-Driven Ansible

## Attendee Prerequisites

None.
